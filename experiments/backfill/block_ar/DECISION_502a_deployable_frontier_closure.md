# 502a Deployable Frontier Closure Decision

## Evidence Since 392a

The current deployable learned frontier is still `392a` at `8/11`.

Post-frontier attempts have now closed the main plausible repair routes:

- Proper-score and objective repairs did not exceed `392a`.
- Critic, density-ratio, source-transport, rank-copula, H60, and learned ensemble routes
  did not exceed `392a`.
- Residual-width policy calibration (`498a`) preserved conditionality but could not move
  level KS beyond the 392a bottleneck.
- Global center-occupancy mapping (`499a`) targeted level law directly but failed
  catastrophically out of split.
- State-conditional ridge center policy (`501a`) was rejected by pre-validation holdout:
  the selected center movement was exactly `alpha=0.0`.

## Current Read

The remaining failed gates are not independent missing knobs:

- Distributional fidelity needs level occupancy movement.
- Coverage and regime coverage need per-cell/per-regime interval allocation.
- Conditionality and mean reversion are thin-margin structural passes in `392a`.

Moving levels enough to affect level KS tends to damage mean reversion, bias, or
coverage. Preserving the 392a center protects conditionality but leaves level KS stuck.

## Decision

Close deployable calibration and wrapper research around `392a`.

The scientifically defensible position is:

```text
base learned deployable model: 392a, 8/11
oracle feasibility:            435a, 11/11, not deployable
policy calibration attempts:   below frontier
```

An `11/11` deployable claim is not supported by the experiments so far. Continuing local
knob-search would be less principled than reporting the frontier honestly.

## Consequence

If the objective remains a publishable, deployable conditional scenario generator, the
next legitimate step is not another 392a wrapper. It is one of:

- accept `392a` as the deployable learned-law result and write the paper around its
  strengths/limitations;
- change the product/test target to distinguish base learned law from risk-policy
  calibration;
- collect or reframe data so future level/regime allocation is identifiable;
- start a genuinely new learned-core program, knowing that prior direct path-law and
  likelihood attempts are already heavily falsified.

Under the current data and suite, no deployable path to `11/11` remains clean enough to
recommend as an immediate local experiment.
